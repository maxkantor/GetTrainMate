# GetTrainMate User Guide

## Understanding the App Navigation

### Dashboard vs Discovery

**Dashboard** (also called "Discover"):
- This is your main feed where you **discover new training partners**
- Located at `/app/discover` or `/app/dashboard`
- Shows profiles of other users you haven't interacted with yet
- Swipe right (Like) or left (Pass) on profiles
- When you both like each other, it's a **Match**! 🎉

**Match Tab**:
- Shows all your **matched users** (people who liked you back)
- Located at `/app/matches`
- View matched profiles and start chatting

**Chat Tab**:
- Shows all your conversations with matched users
- Located at `/app/chat`
- Only appears after you have matches

**Events Tab**:
- Browse and create training events
- Join group activities in your area
- Located at `/app/events`

## How to Use GetTrainMate

### Step 1: Complete Your Profile
1. Click on your avatar in the top right → **Profile**
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** and **City** (required)
   - **Bio**: Tell others about yourself
   - **Sports**: Select all sports you're interested in
   - **Level**: Choose your skill level (beginner/intermediate/advanced/pro)
   - **Goals**: Your fitness goals
   - **Schedule**: When you're available to train
   - **Mode**: TRAIN (fitness partners), VIBE (buddies), or DATE (interested)
   - **Photos**: Upload at least one photo
3. Click **Save Profile**

### Step 2: Discover Training Partners
1. Go to **Dashboard** (or click "Dashboard" in the navigation)
2. You'll see profiles of other users
3. **Swipe right** (Like) or click the **Like** button if you're interested
4. **Swipe left** (Pass) or click the **Pass** button if not interested
5. Keep swiping to see more profiles

### Step 3: View Your Matches
1. Click the **Match** tab
2. See all users who liked you back
3. Click **Start Chat** on any match to begin a conversation

### Step 4: Chat with Matches
1. Click the **Chat** tab
2. Select a conversation from the list
3. Send messages to plan training sessions

### Step 5: Create or Join Events
1. Click the **Events** tab
2. **Create Event**: Click "Create Event" to organize a group activity
3. **Join Event**: Browse events and click "Join" to participate

## Quick Start Checklist

- [ ] Complete your profile with photos and sports
- [ ] Go to Dashboard and start swiping
- [ ] Check Match tab for mutual likes
- [ ] Start chatting with matches
- [ ] Create or join events

## Tips

- **Complete profiles get more matches**: Add photos, bio, and sports
- **Be active**: Like profiles regularly to increase your chances
- **Use Events**: Great way to meet multiple people at once
- **Be specific**: Detailed bios help you find better matches

---

**Note**: If you don't see any profiles in Discovery, you may need to wait for more users to join, or use the seed script to create test users.
